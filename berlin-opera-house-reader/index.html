Berlin Opera House Reader

Open three programme folds. Each one gives you the physical place, the current visitor cue and one calmer next move before you choose an evening.

This is a venue reader, not a booking engine. It does not choose a ticket for you. It helps you put the house, neighbourhood and final production-page check in the right order.

01

Staatsoper

Mitte · Unter den Linden 7

Read the place: this is the house for a day that already has Bebelplatz, Museum Island or Unter den Linden in it.

Check before booking: the official FAQ says German and English surtitles are available but are not readable from every side or rear seat.

Open the official visitor page

02

Deutsche Oper

Charlottenburg · Bismarckstraße 35

Read the place: make it a City West or Charlottenburg evening rather than a rushed extension of a Mitte sightseeing day.

Check before booking: the visitor page lists U2 at Deutsche Oper and accessible U2/U7 access at Bismarckstraße nearby.

Open the official visitor page

03

Komische Oper

Current main venue · Schillertheater

Read the place: Komische Oper lists Schillertheater, Bismarckstraße 110, as its visitor entrance. Older search results can still name Behrenstraße.

Check before booking: use the specific production page as the final address check because a performance can use another location.
