Berlin sick pay clock

Six weeks of full pay sounds like six weeks every time. It is not. For one and the same illness the clock keeps running until enough calendar has passed. Add every spell you have been off with that illness and the board lays them on one timeline, counts the 42 days you have spent, and marks the day the six weeks start again.
