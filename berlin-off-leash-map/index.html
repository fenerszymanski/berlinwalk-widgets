What does your dog need today?

    The rule everywhere else: since 1 January 2019 dogs are on the lead across Berlin unless they are inside one of these areas or the owner holds a Bestandshund or Sachkunde exemption. Distances here are straight lines from the station, not walking routes. Closed-off patches inside a forest area (nature reserves, fenced plots, bathing spots in summer) stay off limits even when the map shows green.
