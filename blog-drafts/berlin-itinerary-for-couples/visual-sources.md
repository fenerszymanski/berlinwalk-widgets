## Editorial checks

- The cover shows a real Berlin handover point, not a promised service or exact route.
- Commons images document real places; no pictured stall, event or opening is presented as current.
- Article captions explain why each place matters to the planning decision.
- Public credits omit internal generation, prompt, workflow and automation provenance.
