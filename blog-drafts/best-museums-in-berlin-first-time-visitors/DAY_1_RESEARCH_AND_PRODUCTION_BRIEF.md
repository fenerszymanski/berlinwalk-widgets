# Day 1 research and production brief — Best Museums in Berlin for First-Time Visitors

## Strategic job

Create the missing first-time decision hub. Existing BerlinWalk pages own Museum Island, pass, Pergamon closure and venue-specific details. This page owns the three-slot decision across a short trip and distributes readers to those owners.

## Audience and intent

- First-time Berlin visitors who search broadly for the best museums but have limited half-days.
- Trip Planner users deciding where museums belong alongside outdoor history, food, hotel location and bookings.
- Do not promise a generic top-ten list. The core decision is interest, time, cost and geography.

## Current factual basis — recheck before publish

- Neues Museum: Tuesday–Sunday 10:00–18:00, Monday closed; advance planning via the official visitor and ticket pages.
- Jewish Museum Berlin: Tuesday–Sunday 10:00–18:00; core exhibition free and a time slot recommended.
- Topography of Terror: daily 10:00–20:00, free; accurately called a documentation centre on the former SS and Gestapo headquarters site.
- Pergamonmuseum: current closure/reopening status must be checked on the official Museum Island page at publishing.

## Selected widget

`berlin-museum-three-slot-builder`: trip length, available half-days, interests, paid/free preference, pace and hotel side give exactly three distributed slots plus one alternate. It must not use playful ranking for sensitive history or schedule all visits on one day.
