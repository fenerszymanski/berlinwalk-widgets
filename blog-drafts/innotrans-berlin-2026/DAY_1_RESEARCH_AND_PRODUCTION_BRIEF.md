- [Official visitor information](https://www.innotrans.de/en/visit) lists Expo Center Berlin, 14055 Berlin, plus current visitor services, app and hall-plan guidance.
- Do not add ticket prices, qualifying criteria, entrance gates, specific transit routes or exhibitor claims without a final official check.
