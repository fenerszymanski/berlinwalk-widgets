# Berlin Food Week 2026: source notes

Checked: 22 August 2026. These notes are internal publishing support, not body copy.

## Verified facts used in the body

- Berlin Food Week 2026 runs from 5 to 11 October.
  - Source: [Berlin Food Week home page](https://www.berlinfoodweek.de/)
- House of Food is listed for 9 and 10 October at BIKINI BERLIN, from 10:00 to 20:00 on Friday and Saturday, with free entry.
  - The organiser describes a market for discovering, tasting and buying food products, with producers and brands on site. Free entry identifies a public anchor, not a blanket access guarantee for each activity.
  - Source: [House of Food 2026](https://www.berlinfoodweek.de/event/hof-event-1/)
- Berlin Food Night is listed for 5 October at AchtBerlin and says registration is by invitation only.
  - Its organiser framing is a strategy dialogue on Longevity; that framing belongs only to this one listing.
  - Source: [Berlin Food Night 2026](https://www.berlinfoodweek.de/event/bfn2026/)

## Scope boundary kept in the body

- The Longevity framing is attached to the Berlin Food Night page only. The body does not describe Berlin Food Week as a Longevity festival.
- The body does not call most of the festival industry-only. It explains that access differs by individual listing.
