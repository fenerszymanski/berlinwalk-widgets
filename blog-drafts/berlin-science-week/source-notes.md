# Berlin Science Week 2026: source notes

Checked: 22 August 2026. These notes are internal publishing support, not body copy.

## Verified facts used in the body

- Berlin Science Week 2026 runs from 1 to 10 November.
- The 2026 theme is `In Touch`. The official framing connects the personal side of science, the people behind research and the connections it creates.
- The official site says more than 200 organisations join each year and describes hundreds of free events across the city.
  - Source: [Berlin Science Week home page](https://berlinscienceweek.com/)
- HKW, Haus der Kulturen der Welt, is the 2026 Festival Centre.
  - Source: [Berlin Science Week at HKW](https://berlinscienceweek.com/berlin-science-week-2026-haus-der-kulturen-der-welt)
- The official visitor page says exact venues, dates and event details will be announced with the programme launch in September 2026, and that event sign-ups open with that launch.
  - Source: [Visitor information](https://berlinscienceweek.com/visitor-information)
- Falling Walls lists its Science Summit separately for 6 to 9 November 2026.
  - Source: [Falling Walls FAQ](https://falling-walls.com/frequently-asked-questions)

## Scope boundary kept in the body

- The body makes no superlative ranking, broad language assumption or event-total claim beyond the official wording used above.
- The body does not infer demand. It only says to check each official listing after the programme launch.
- Falling Walls is treated as a separate programme with overlapping dates, not as an automatic part of Berlin Science Week.

## Programme-dependent facts intentionally not asserted

- No daily event density, personalised optimisation, individual venue, event language, ticket, registration or accessibility claim is asserted without an individual live listing.
- Any specific event recommendation needs a current official programme page at publication time.
