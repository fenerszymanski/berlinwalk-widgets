# TAG DER CLUBKULTUR Berlin 2026: source notes

Checked: 22 August 2026. These notes are internal publishing support, not body copy.

## Verified facts used in the body

- The 2026 edition is the seventh TAG DER CLUBKULTUR. The Clubcommission's announcement gives the theme as `STAY CORE` and says 32 clubs and collectives will be recognised through the culture prize.
  - Source: [Clubcommission Berlin announcement, 15 June 2026](https://www.clubcommission.de/tag-der-clubkultur-2026-startet-bewerbungsphase-fuer-kulturpreis-der-berliner-clubkultur/)
- The official programme says the festival begins on 3 October 2026, has more than 100 events, and lists club nights, open-airs, concerts, performances, exhibitions, workshops, talks and film screenings as formats.
  - Source: [TAG DER CLUBKULTUR programme 2026](https://tagderclubkultur.berlin/en/programm/)

## Date treatment

- The Clubcommission announcement and the current official programme page both say 3 to 11 October 2026.
- The body says only that the festival begins on 3 October and runs across a festival week. Recheck the official programme before publication in case the schedule changes.

## Programme-dependent facts intentionally not asserted

- No individual event, artist, venue, ticket, door-policy, accessibility, timing or capacity detail is asserted.
- No claim says that entry is easy, that events are mostly not club nights, or that a named venue will have a particular format.
