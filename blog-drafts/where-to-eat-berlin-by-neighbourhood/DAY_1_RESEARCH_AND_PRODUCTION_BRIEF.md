# Day 1 research and production brief — Where to Eat in Berlin by Neighbourhood

## Strategic job

Own the decision before individual restaurant research: one dinner neighbourhood per night, with a transit anchor and a realistic after-dinner move. Do not compete with existing cuisine-, diet-, market- or late-night-specific owner pages.

## Audience and intent

- First-time visitors deciding where a dinner evening belongs in a short trip.
- Visitors with hotel, museum, fair or arrival constraints who need a compact night rather than a top-ten restaurant list.
- High Trip Planner relevance: the choice depends on the rest of the day and hotel side.

## Search / cannibalisation guard

- Exact title and slug were checked against the 277-post Wix Blog snapshot on 3 August 2026: no exact collision.
- This page must not rank restaurants, give fixed opening hours, or replace the Markthalle, Turkish market, Vietnamese-food, vegan, halal or late-night owner pages.
- Primary promise: geography and evening shape, not "best food in Berlin".

## Selected widget

`dinner-neighbourhood-picker`: hotel side, number of nights, mood and walking energy produce one compact evening per night: transit anchor -> dining street/market cluster -> nearby after-dinner landmark. It must tell readers to check live venue details.
