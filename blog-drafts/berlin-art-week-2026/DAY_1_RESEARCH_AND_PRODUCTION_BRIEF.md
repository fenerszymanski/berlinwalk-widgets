## Confirmed facts, checked 3 August 2026

- [Berlin Art Week official overview](https://berlinartweek.de/en/about-berlin-art-week/) confirms **9 to 13 September 2026**, with exhibitions, performances, film screenings and talks across the city.
- The same official outline currently describes citywide openings on Wednesday; Positions Berlin Art Fair at Tempelhof Airport and Gallery Night on Thursday; independent spaces and Featured Night on Friday; Open Houses without prior registration and limited Discovering Collections! tours on Saturday; and talks, Garten programming and guided tours on Sunday.
- Individual venues, access rules and times remain live-programme information. The post must keep [official locations](https://berlinartweek.de/en/locations/) as the source of truth and must not add invented dates, entry conditions or ticket prices.
