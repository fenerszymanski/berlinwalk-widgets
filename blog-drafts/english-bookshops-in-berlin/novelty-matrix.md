## Proposed tool fingerprint

- **Primary mechanic:** reveal a real bookshop stop directly on a geographic line, then move a bookmark along the map to compare a different part of Berlin.
- **Input model:** a deliberate click on one map marker or one line-stop button, not a scored preference form or a right/wrong answer.
- **State progression:** map focus changes, the selected marker pulses, a location sheet opens, and the bookmark moves to the chosen stop.
- **Output model:** one place-and-purpose pairing plus an official-page check, not a route score, countdown, quiz result or booking recommendation.
- **Visual grammar:** a photographic deep-green reading desk around a real Leaflet/OSM map, brass bookmark marker and compact location sheet.

The nearest same-day tool is Berlin Opera House Reader at 1/5 because both reveal place information. Its folding-programme layout, three parallel panels and venue reading mechanic are distinct. No tool reaches the 4/5 rejection threshold; the three same-day primary mechanics and visual grammars are distinct.
