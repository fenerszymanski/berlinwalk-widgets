## Editorial decision

**Public title:** Morning or Afternoon Walking Tour in Berlin: Which Time Fits Your Day?
**SEO title:** Morning or Afternoon Walking Tour in Berlin?

**Slug:** `morning-or-afternoon-walking-tour-berlin`
**Focus keyword:** `Berlin walking tour morning or afternoon`
**Search intent:** Commercial decision support for a visitor considering the currently verified BerlinWalk Free Walking Tour.
**Category:** Tourist Tips

## SERP answerability

- Rejected form: “What time is the BerlinWalk tour?” The live calendar answers that.
- Accepted form: which available time fits a visitor's arrival, hard stop, weather, daylight and next plan.
- Why it earns the click: a search snippet cannot intersect two personal time boundaries with date-specific tour windows or show the before/after day trade-off.

## Offer and claim boundaries

- One primary offer only: the live BerlinWalk Free Walking Tour booking page.
- Verified live claims: free to reserve, tip based, about 2 hours, World Clock start, Hackescher Markt finish.
- The calendar showed 11:30 and 15:30 on a currently bookable date. The public copy must say availability is date-specific and tell readers to check the live calendar.
- Never imply the route follows the Berlin Wall line.

## Widget decision

Selected: `berlin-tour-time-window`, a dual-boundary dayline. It takes the earliest realistic World Clock arrival and latest required free time, then intersects both with the current 11:30-13:30 and 15:30-17:30 planning bands. It can return morning, afternoon, both or neither without a fake winner.

Rejected: an analogue orbit control was less accessible; a light/heat image lens would overstate conditions without a live date.

## Visual and credit plan

- Five authentic BerlinWalk gallery photographs form the article sequence.
- The World Clock photograph is also the tool's own primary visual.
- The article owns one native Wix `COLLAPSIBLE_LIST` Image credits disclosure, default closed.
- The widget does not repeat or point to article-image credits.
- The only generated visual is the small BerlinTools icon; its internal provenance must not appear publicly.

## Video decision

No video. It would not improve the time-window decision enough to justify another source, embed and QA surface.
