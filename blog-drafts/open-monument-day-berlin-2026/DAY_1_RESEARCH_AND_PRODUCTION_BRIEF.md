## SERP answerability verdict

- Rejected short form: `When is Open Monument Day Berlin?` It is a one-line calendar answer.
- Accepted form: choose and book a hidden site using a district, time, registration and access plan.
- Why it earns the click: Google cannot turn a thousand distributed programme entries into a realistic visitor itinerary or registration back-up strategy.

## Confirmed facts, checked 3 August 2026

- [Berlin's heritage authority](https://www.berlin.de/landesdenkmalamt/veranstaltungen/tag-des-offenen-denkmals/) confirms **12 and 13 September 2026** and the 2026 theme `NetzWERKE: Denkmale & Infrastruktur`.
- The authority currently describes about 350 monuments and around 1,000 activities, generally free, across Berlin.
- The official [programme search](https://denkmaltag.berlin.de/suche) has usable filters for district, day, time, themes, foreign language, registration/no registration, wheelchair access, indoor access and day-only access.
- English-language events are also surfaced by the official [English programme overview](https://www.berlin.de/landesdenkmalamt/veranstaltungen/tag-des-offenen-denkmals/english-programme-1570034.php).

1. `heritage-booking-ladder` - asks for day, district/interest, language and access needs; ranks the exact booking-first search followed by a same-area no-registration back-up search. **Selected.**
2. Static map of favourite monuments - rejected because the official programme has live entries and the article must not invent available sites.
3. Ticket-price calculator - rejected because the weekend is generally free and the real decision is access, not price.
