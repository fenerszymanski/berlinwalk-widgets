## Visual QA

- Cover: clear front elevation, no crowd or event-specific claim.
- Deutsche Oper: recognisable exterior with a deliberately different modern visual grammar from the Staatsoper cover.
- Schillertheater: selected over a later image carrying a historical Staatsoper banner, so the photo cannot imply that the Staatsoper still performs there.
- Auditorium: useful only for the seat-and-surtitles section; it is not used to claim a current production or seat availability.
