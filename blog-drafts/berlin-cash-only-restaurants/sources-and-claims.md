# Sources and claims — Cash-Only Restaurants in Berlin

- **Deutsche Bundesbank, 17 June 2026:** cashless payments made up 55% of recorded 2025 purchases, but the study still found cashless payment unavailable for some in-person purchases. Public claim: Berlin visitors should carry both a card and a small cash reserve, rather than treating either as universal.
- **visitBerlin FAQ, checked 18 August 2026:** cash and cards are both used in Berlin; small restaurants, snack bars, bakeries, street vendors and markets can still be cash-first or cash-only. Public claim: check the counter sign or ask before ordering; avoid claiming a named independent venue's current policy.
- **BerlinWalk booking page, checked 18 August 2026:** BerlinWalk Free Walking Tour is live. Public claim: it is a free walking tour; do not name a required tip, price, availability or booking promise.

## Editorial exclusions

- No cash amount, card brand or individual-payment promise is presented as universal.
- No restaurant, market or street-food stall is identified as currently cash-only.
- The tool is not a bank, fee, exchange-rate, ATM, reservation or tip calculator.
