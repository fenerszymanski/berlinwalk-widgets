# Christmas Garden Berlin 2026: Pick the Right Entry Time Before You Leave

Local research notes only. Not for the Wix body.

Checked: 22 August 2026, Europe/Berlin.

## Verified facts used in the body

- Christmas Garden Berlin runs at Botanischer Garten Berlin from 18 November 2026 to 10 January 2027.
- Normal listed hours are Monday to Thursday and Sunday, 16:20 to 21:00. Last entry to the grounds is 19:20 and last start on the circular route is 19:45.
- Friday and Saturday, 18 December 2026 to 2 January 2027, have listed hours of 16:20 to 22:00. Last entry is 20:20 and last route start is 20:45.
- Listed closed dates are 23, 24 and 30 November; 7, 24 and 31 December; and 4 and 5 January.
- The organiser describes 20-minute entry windows and names the two access points: Königin-Luise-Straße 6-8 and Unter den Eichen 5-10, with the stated public-transport stops.
