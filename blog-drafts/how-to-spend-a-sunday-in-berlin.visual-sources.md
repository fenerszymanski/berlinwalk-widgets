require attribution. The reader-facing credit block lives in the article's own
Ricos rich content as a single default-closed COLLAPSIBLE_LIST ("Image
credits"), built from `scripts/article-image-credits-sunday-in-berlin-20260725.mjs`.
No article credit appears in the widget or the /tools page.

in `images/raw/` (gitignored) and can be rebuilt from the source URLs below.

## Rejected / not used
- `Tempelhofer Feld (16895371610).jpg` — striking but desolate (lone radar
  beacon, no people); did not carry the warm Sunday-outdoors feeling. Rejected.
- Higher-res Mauerpark-flea images existed but the cover was chosen for the
  people + TV Tower composition that reads instantly as a Berlin Sunday.

## Selection rationale
Arc follows a real Sunday: flea market morning (cover + stall) → Mauerpark
karaoke afternoon → grey-day museum option (Museum Island) → outdoors
(Landwehrkanal, Weißensee lido). Every image is a real place named in the body.
