# Visual sources — where-to-eat-near-brandenburg-gate

All images are Wikimedia Commons, resized to 1600px long edge, q86, EXIF-orientation baked
(Pillow `ImageOps.exif_transpose`). Reader-facing Image credits ship as one native Wix
Ricos COLLAPSIBLE_LIST disclosure inside the article (default closed). Raw originals kept
locally under `raw/`, not committed.

## Final set (5 images, cover = 01)

1. **01-brandenburg-gate-pariser-platz.jpg** (cover) — Crowds and a costumed photo-op tout on
   Pariser Platz in front of the Brandenburg Gate. Sets up the tourist-trap scene.
   - Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Berlin_Brandenburger_Tor_BW_5.jpg
   - Author: Berthold Werner · Licence: CC BY-SA 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)

2. **02-berlin-doner-kebab.jpg** — A chicken döner with salad and feta in hand, a Berlin staple.
   The honest few-euro alternative to the Gate's photo-menu restaurants.
   - Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hahnchen_Doner_mit_Gemuse_at_Mustafas_Gemusekebap.jpg
   - Author: Bodhisattwa · Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

3. **03-berlin-currywurst-done-right.jpg** — A paper tray of currywurst with the little wooden fork.
   - Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Original_Berliner_Currywurst.jpg
   - Author: Richard Mischau, Britta Mischau · Licence: CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

4. **04-cafe-am-neuen-see-tiergarten.jpg** — The lakeside beer garden at Café am Neuen See in the
   Tiergarten on a summer day. The sit-down escape, open daily.
   - Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Caf%C3%A9_am_Neuen_See.jpg
   - Author: Lear 21 · Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)

5. **05-tiergarten-neuer-see.jpg** — Rowing boats on the Neuer See in the Tiergarten, with the beer
   garden at the right. The free picnic-in-the-park move, a two-minute walk from the Gate.
   - Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Berlin_Tiergarten_Neuer_See_2.jpg
   - Author: Schlaier · Licence: Public domain

## Rejected
- "I Love Currywurst" Buddy Bear at Pariser Platz (Roy Zuo, CC BY-SA 4.0): rotated + a brand
  advertising mascot (Curry Wolf). Not neutral.
- "Altberliner Imbiss am Kutschi" stand (Dirk Ingo Franke, CC BY 3.0): shows a €1.20 currywurst
  price board from 2012, which would mislead readers on current prices.

## Facts checked on 20 August 2026
- 7-day BVG ticket discontinued 1 Jan 2026 (not used in this post, noted for accuracy elsewhere).
- Café am Neuen See open daily ~08:00-23:00 (visitBerlin / official).
- Mall of Berlin food court on Leipziger Platz, Mon-Sat 10:00-21:00, closed Sunday.
- Galeries Lafayette Berlin (Friedrichstraße) CLOSED end of July 2024 — deliberately not cited.
