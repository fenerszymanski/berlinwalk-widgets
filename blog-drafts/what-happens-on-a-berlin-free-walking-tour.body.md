## What a free walking tour in Berlin actually is

A free walking tour is a guided walk you can join without paying up front. At the end, you decide what it was worth and tip the guide accordingly. That is the whole model. There is no ticket, no fixed price, and no obligation to pay a set amount. If you have never done one, I explain exactly [how tip-based tours work](https://www.berlinwalk.com/post/what-is-a-free-walking-tour-how-tip-based-tours-actually-work) in a separate guide.

What it is not: it is not a bus, it is not rushed, and it is not a sales trap. It is two hours on foot through the historic core of the former East Berlin, at a comfortable pace, on mostly flat ground.

## The two hours, stop by stop

The walk starts at the World Clock on Alexanderplatz and ends about two hours later at Hackescher Markt, roughly a kilometre and a half west, through thirteen stops. As the scrubber above shows, the arc is deliberate: we begin where East Berlin built its showcase square on top of the medieval city, work through the oldest quarter and the Cold War statues, cross to Museum Island, and finish in the courtyards where the old East feels alive again.

**The pace is built for looking, not marching.** Nobody is dragged. If you want the fuller written version of the route, I wrote it up as [twelve stops through Berlin's ancient core](https://www.berlinwalk.com/post/12-stops-through-berlin-s-ancient-core-what-you-ll-see-on-our-free-walking-tour).

## How the tipping actually works

This is the part people worry about, so let me be plain. At the end of the walk I say thank you, and you tip what the two hours were worth to you, in cash or by card. There is no cornering, no guilt, no set figure announced to the group.

If you want a sense of the normal range before you go, I cover it honestly in [tipping in Berlin](https://www.berlinwalk.com/post/tipping-in-berlin). The short version: tip what felt fair for two hours of someone's work and local knowledge, the same way you would judge any service in the city.

## Who it is for, and who it is not

A free walking tour is a great fit if you are new to Berlin, if you want the historic centre explained rather than just photographed, and if you are happy on your feet for two hours. Families do it, solo travellers do it, and it works well on a first day when you are getting your bearings.

It is a poor fit if you cannot walk for two hours, if you want to go inside the museums (we walk the edges of Museum Island, not the galleries), or if you want a private, tailored experience. **My honest advice: if mobility is the issue, message me before booking and I will tell you straight whether the route works for you**, rather than have you find out halfway along.

## Where and when it meets

The walk meets at the World Clock on Alexanderplatz, which is one of the easiest landmarks in the city to find and reachable by U-Bahn, S-Bahn, tram and bus. Details of the exact spot are on the [meeting point page](https://www.berlinwalk.com/meeting-point), and current dates are on the [walk's booking page](https://www.berlinwalk.com/free-berlin-walking-tour).

Alexanderplatz sits in what was East Berlin, and the official city tourism board, [visitBerlin](https://www.visitberlin.de/en), has the wider context on the area if you want to read around it. But you do not need to prepare anything. Turn up at the World Clock, and the two hours you just scrubbed through are the two hours you get.
