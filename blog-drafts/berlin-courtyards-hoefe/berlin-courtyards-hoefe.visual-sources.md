# Visual sources — berlin-courtyards-hoefe

All images sourced from Wikimedia Commons. Masters optimized to 1600px long
edge, JPEG q86, uploaded to Wix Media. Raw originals kept local only under
`images/raw/` (gitignored); rebuild from the source URLs below.

Attribution required for the three CC BY-SA files. The two CC0 files need no
attribution but are credited anyway in the article's native "Image credits"
disclosure for consistency (one credit entry per image = 5).

## 1. Cover — `cover-hackesche-hoefe-facade.jpg`
- Subject: Hackesche Höfe, Court I (Hof 1), August Endell's Jugendstil glazed
  tile facade lit at night, wet cobbles, "Kino" sign.
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Berlin_Hackesche_H%C3%B6fe2.jpg
- Author: Marek Śliwecki
- Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0
- Why: The single most iconic, instantly readable "Hackesche Höfe" image;
  strong at card-crop size. Editorial-grade cover.

## 2. `hackesche-hoefe-zum-hof-iv.jpg`
- Subject: "Zum Hof IV." courtyard-number sign under red autumn vine leaves.
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Berlin,_Hackesche_H%C3%B6fe_--_2011_--_2438.jpg
- Author: Dietmar Rabich
- Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0
- Why: Explains the eight-numbered-courtyard system in one atmospheric detail.

## 3. `haus-schwarzenberg-street-art.jpg`
- Subject: Playful googly-eyes paste-up on the graffiti-covered wall of Haus
  Schwarzenberg's courtyard.
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Haus_Schwarzenberg_Innenhof_Streetart_Berlin-Mitte_12.jpg
- Author: Singlespeedfahrer
- Licence: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Why: Shows the gritty, unrestored contrast next door to the polished Höfe,
  non-political and friendly. (A neighbouring paste-up shot with a gun/political
  stickers was rejected as politically charged.)

## 4. `rosenhoefe-arcade.jpg`
- Subject: Rosenhöfe — the green wrought-iron arcade and rose garden, with the
  TV Tower rising behind the roofline.
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blick_in_die_Rosenh%C3%B6fe,_Berlin-Mitte,_2026-03-23.jpg
- Author: Nylki
- Licence: CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Why: Distinct green-arcade design, and the TV Tower ties it to central Berlin.

## 5. `sophienstrasse-courtyard.jpg`
- Subject: A quiet, sunlit residential courtyard off Sophienstraße with café
  tables and a passage back to the street.
- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Courtyard_Sophienhof_Sophienstrasse_19.jpg
- Author: Helge Høifødt
- Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0 — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0
- Why: The calm, ordinary-Berlin courtyard beat — the payoff after the busy Höfe.

## Rejected
- `Haus_Schwarzenberg_Innenhof_Streetart_Berlin-Mitte_02.jpg` (CC0): strong
  texture but includes a gun paste-up and politically charged stickers — reject
  per the protest/politically-charged image rule.
- Several leafy inner-courtyard shots of the Hackesche Höfe (Oktober 2022,
  Werner100359 01): pleasant but read as a generic green yard, weak as cover.
