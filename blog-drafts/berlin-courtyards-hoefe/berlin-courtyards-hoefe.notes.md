## Verified facts (2026-07-24)
- Hackesche Höfe: opened 23 Sep 1906; architect **Kurt Berndt**, Art Nouveau facade of Court I by **August Endell**; eight interconnected courtyards; largest single courtyard complex in Germany; listed since 1972; nationalised/neglected in GDR; restored from 1993 (~80M DM). Rosenthaler Str. 40–41. Public access ~7:00–23:00; front courtyards always open, residential ones close at night. (hackesche-hoefe.de, visitBerlin, berlin.de, en.wikipedia)
- Haus Schwarzenberg: Rosenthaler Str. 39; artists' association since 1995; deliberately unrestored street-art passage; holds Anne Frank Zentrum, Museum Otto Weidt's Workshop for the Blind, Kino Central, Neurotitan, Monster Kabinett. (haus-schwarzenberg.org, annefrank.de, berlin.de)
- Rosenhöfe: Rosenthaler Str. 40 through-passage; 1990s redesign, green wrought iron + rose garden; connects toward Sophienstraße.
- Sophienstraße / Sophie-Gips-Höfe: Sophienstr. 19–21; restored (not rebuilt) 18th–19th c. street; Sophie-Gips-Höfe = former sewing-machine factory, elegant/quiet. (Did NOT claim a specific open gallery — Sammlung Hoffmann closed 2016.)
- Heckmann-Höfe: Oranienburger Str. 32 / Auguststr. 9; restored 19th-c. courtyard; **Bonbonmacherei** traditional hand-made candy workshop, glass kitchen, open only a few days/week and takes a summer break (told readers to check). (visitBerlin)
- Transit: **S Hackescher Markt = S-Bahn only (S3/S5/S7/S9) + trams M1/M4/M5/M6. NO U-Bahn there.** Nearest U-Bahn = Weinmeisterstraße (U8), ~5 min. (Corrected an incorrect "U-Bahn stops at Hackescher Markt" claim seen in one search summary.)
