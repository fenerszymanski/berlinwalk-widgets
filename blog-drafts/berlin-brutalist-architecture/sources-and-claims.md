| Corbusierhaus was built 1956-58 for Interbau 1957 and designed by Le Corbusier. | [Berlin Monument Database](https://denkmaldatenbank.berlin.de/daobj.php?obj_dok_nr=09040533) | Described as post-war modernism, not called Brutalist. |
