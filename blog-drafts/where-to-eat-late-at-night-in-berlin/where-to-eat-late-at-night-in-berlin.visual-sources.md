## Rejected candidates
- Sonnenallee Berlin-Neukölln 2020-06-26 (Leonhard Lenz, CC0) - strong and
  free, but a flat daytime street shot; weaker than the night images for a
  late-night article, and a second Kreuzberg/Neukölln street shot added little.
- Konnopke Imbiss 2014 (Orderinchaos, CC BY-SA 4.0) - good imbiss shot but
  daytime and redundant with the stronger Curry 36 currywurst image.
- Spätkauf in Berlin-Kreuzberg (Nicolas Bouliane) - fine, but the
  "Late Night Shop" sign is more on-topic and reads instantly at card size.

## Cover judgement
Cover is the Berlin döner + beer photo: appetising, instantly readable at
listing-card size, and it names the single food most tourists actually eat late
in Berlin. Two Kottbusser Tor night images are kept on purpose: one wide skyline
for the "late food follows the stations" thesis near the top, one station-mouth
shot for the "getting home" beat, because Kotti is the article's protagonist
location.

## No AI visuals used
No AI-generated images in the article body. The BerlinTools icon is generated
separately through the approved non-paid visual route.
