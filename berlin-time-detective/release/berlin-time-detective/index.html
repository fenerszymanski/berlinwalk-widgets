    Case guide

    One clear step at a time

    Five missions connect thirteen real stops. Every mission follows the same short loop:

    1. Read the task. It appears at the top of the active mission.
    2. Choose or arrange the evidence. A wrong choice records an observation but never locks the game.
    3. Collect the clue and continue. The yellow button opens the next mission in route order.

    The field map shows route geography. It does not claim that documentary photographs share one viewpoint.
