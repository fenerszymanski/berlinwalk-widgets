Data: weekly lab-confirmed influenza cases reported in Berlin, Robert Koch Institute, CC BY 4.0, read on 21 September 2026. Each winter counts reporting weeks 35 to 20. Lab-confirmed cases are only the people who were tested, so the real wave is larger, but its timing is what matters here. The 2020/21 and 2021/22 seasons are left out: the first had almost no flu under pandemic rules and the second peaked in May. Dates are for the 2026/27 winter. This is timing guidance, not medical advice.
