Berlin's Landes-Immissionsschutzgesetz protects the night between 22:00 and 06:00, and Sundays and public holidays for the whole day. Building sites count 20:00 to 07:00 as night. A house rulebook is a private agreement, so it binds you through the flat, not through the city. Rules checked on 6 September 2026.
