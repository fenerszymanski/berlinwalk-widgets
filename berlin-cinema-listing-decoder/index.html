Berlin cinemas that reliably show films you can follow

A short, honest shortlist. Programmes change, so always check the label on the day.

Mitte, Karl-Marx-Allee
Kino International

Berlin's most beautiful old cinema, a 1963 modernist hall with a neon sign. Regular OV and OmeU screenings and premieres.

Mitte, Rosa-Luxemburg-Platz
Babylon Mitte

Independent repertory house. Classics, retrospectives and new arthouse, much of it in the original language with subtitles.

Mitte, near Hackescher Markt
Hackesche Höfe Kino

An arthouse cinema tucked in the courtyards. Original-language programme, and a two-minute walk from where my walking tour ends.

Charlottenburg, Kantstrasse
Delphi Lux and Delphi Filmpalast

New releases and festival films, many in OV or OmeU. Handy if you are staying in the west.

Across the city
Yorck cinemas

A group of a dozen screens (including Babylon Kreuzberg and Neues Off) that lists all its original-version showings in one place.

How to see what is on tonight

Two local sites collect every original-version screening across Berlin, so you can filter by cinema and language before you leave the hotel.

ovkino.de ov-berlin.de yorck.de (OV filter)
