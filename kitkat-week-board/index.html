The KitKat week: same room, different rules

KitKatClub is not one party. Each night is run by a different organiser, and each organiser publishes its own door time and its own dress rule. Pick the night you can actually go and read what that night asks for.

No street clothes at all No jeans or T-shirts Smart casual accepted No evening party

Door times and dress rules as published by the club and its event organisers, checked on 27 August 2026. Fridays rotate between organisers, so the Friday you land on decides the rule. Night services read from live BVG departures at U Heinrich-Heine-Straße on the same date. The club publishes no closing times, so the bars show when doors open, not when the party ends.
