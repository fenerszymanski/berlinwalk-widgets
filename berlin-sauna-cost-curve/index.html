Berlin Sauna Day Board

Three open saunas, three completely different products. One is a long open band, one stops admitting anybody ninety minutes before it closes, one only sells fixed two hour slots. Here they are on one day, priced on the same basket: a two hour visit with the sauna, starting at the hour you turn up.

Tap or click anywhere on the board to move the hour. The board takes keyboard focus, so the left and right arrow keys work too.
