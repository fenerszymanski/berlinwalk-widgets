# Source notes — Berlin Food Week access classifier

## Confirmed 2026 facts used

- Berlin Food Week is scheduled for **5–11 October 2026**.
- **House of Food** is listed for **9–10 October 2026**, **10:00–20:00**, at **BIKINI Berlin**, with **free entry**.
- **Berlin Food Night** is listed for **5 October 2026** and says that participation is **by invitation only**.

## Access model

The interface deliberately uses exactly five classes: **free entry**, **reservation**, **ticket**, **invite-only**, and **check details**. Only House of Food and Berlin Food Night are specific confirmed 2026 examples. Reservation and ticket are conditional classes that require the exact listing’s own wording; they are not claims about an unnamed 2026 event.

## Deliberate boundaries

- No festival-wide public/private, industry-only, ticketed or reservation rule is asserted.
- The tool never treats an enquiry as an invitation, or a Food Week name as proof of entry.
- It does not name unverified programme items, prices, venues or availability.
