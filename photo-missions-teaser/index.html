The station that lost everything but one door.

Anhalter Bahnhof was once Berlin's grandest terminus. Almost none of it survived. Find the one piece still standing.

Why this stop

Anhalter Bahnhof was continental Europe's largest train station before the war. Bombing gutted it, and in 1959 most of the ruin was demolished rather than repaired, a decision that became its own piece of Cold War history. The portal on your card is what a visitor is left to stand in front of today.
