When your Berlin ride home goes dark

Berlin is far enough north that the evening light drains faster here than in Munich or Stuttgart, and most of the change lands inside a few weeks. Pick the two times you actually travel and the board below shows the dates your own commute crosses into the hours when lights are required by law.

Police-recorded accidents in Berlin in 2025. September sits above August and October, which is the month the evenings go dark while the number of people still riding stays high.

Sun times are calculated for the centre of Berlin and cross-checked against published almanac values; they shift by about a minute across the city. Twilight here means civil twilight, the window when the sun is up to six degrees below the horizon and the sky still holds some light. Gate hours are the published monthly hours for Tempelhofer Feld.
