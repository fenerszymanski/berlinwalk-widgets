Berlin WBS income ladder

The Wohnungsamt does not test your salary. It tests what is left after an employee allowance, up to three ten percent deductions and any allowances, and then compares that to five Berlin lines. Walk the three steps and the ladder below shows how far your gross falls before it is measured.

Who counts as your household

A flatshare cannot apply together and separate certificates cannot be pooled, so if you are three friends looking for one flat, each of you is a one person household here.
