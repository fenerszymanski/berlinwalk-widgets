Berlin Co-op Share Payback Plotter

A Berlin housing cooperative asks for share money before you get the keys, and that money sits with the cooperative for as long as you stay. This plots the two routes side by side: the share payment plus a low cooperative rent, against the open-market rent you would otherwise sign. Checked on 23 September 2026.

Step 1 of 3

The flat you are aiming at

Share money is set by floor area, not by what you earn. The ladder below is the published one at Wohnungsgenossenschaft Treptower Park eG, where one share is 256 euros. Other Berlin cooperatives set their own, so treat it as a realistic starting figure and overwrite it in the next step if you have a real number.

The living space on the contract, not the room you sleep in.
Step 2 of 3

The two rents, and the money you park

Use net cold rent per square metre on both lines so the comparison is fair. The Berliner Mietspiegel 2026, in force since 28 May 2026, puts the Berlin average for existing tenancies at 7.71 euros. Member companies of the BBU, which is where most cooperative stock sits, averaged 7.10 euros on 30 June 2025. A new open-market contract in Berlin usually costs a good deal more than either, so put in the figure from the listing you are actually looking at.
