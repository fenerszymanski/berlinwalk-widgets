    What a private walk does not change

    The route stays in the historic centre. I do not stitch Charlottenburg Palace, the East Side Gallery and Mitte into the same two hours, because that day is mostly transport and the walk stops being a walk. If your group wants places that far apart, book the centre properly and keep the rest of the day separate.

    The free tip-based walk keeps running on its fixed days with a mixed group. A private walk can change the emphasis and pace inside the central route after confirmation. Check opening hours, tickets and access needs separately before you fix the day.
