- The board is a selected six-date calendar aid, not a complete autumn festival list.
- Exact cinema, screening time, ticket terms, availability, spoken language, subtitles and any introduction, discussion or Q&A must be checked on the individual live listing.
- No festival-wide English, OV, OmU, accessibility or ticket claim is made.
