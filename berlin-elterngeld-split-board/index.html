Plan your Elterngeld split before the form goes in the post

Set how many months each of you takes and what a full Basiselterngeld month is worth, and the board lays the plan out over your child's Lebensmonate, checks it against the rules, and adds up what it pays.

Parent 1 (birth parent)

Months 1 and 2 are normally spoken for by Mutterschutz, which is counted as Basiselterngeld whether you apply for it or not.

Parent 2

Two months here are what unlocks the 13th and 14th month for the household. Nobody else can use them.

Basiselterngeld a month 1,800
Basis months 2
ElterngeldPlus months 0

Two, three or four extra ElterngeldPlus months each, on top of the 14, and only while you both work 24 to 32 hours a week in the same months.
