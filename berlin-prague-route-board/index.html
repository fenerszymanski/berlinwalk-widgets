    The bus, for comparison

    • FlixBus lists around 25 departures a day from Berlin, with a fastest scheduled run of 3 hours 40 minutes. RegioJet runs the same corridor from Südkreuz.
    • Buses leave from several points across Berlin and land at Florenc, which is two metro stops out from the old town rather than a walk from it.
    • The bus is the one that keeps running when the railway does not, and it takes the motorway, so its risk is traffic rather than trackwork.
