Berlin Market Week

Berlin's weekly markets run on fixed weekdays and most of them are packed away by mid-afternoon. Give it the date you are here and every market trading that day is drawn on one clock line, so you can see how late you can turn up. Tap two of them to check whether the hours actually overlap.
