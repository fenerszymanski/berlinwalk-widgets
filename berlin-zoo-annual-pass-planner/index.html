Prices as published by Zoo, Aquarium and Tierpark Berlin, checked on 28 September 2026. Online day tickets start at the lower figure and cost more closer to the day; the gate price is the upper one. Card holders pay half the regular day price at the two places their card does not cover. Family cards need everyone at the same postal address and proof for each child. The subscription renews itself and runs at least 24 months; reduced cards have no subscription except for disabled visitors. All prices include the optional species-protection contribution.
