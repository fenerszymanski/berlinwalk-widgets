# Christmas Garden Leave-By Ladder

Local source notes for the `christmas-garden-closed-night-calendar` widget. These notes are not reader-facing copy.

Checked: 22 August 2026, Europe/Berlin.

## Confirmed facts used

- Christmas Garden Berlin at Botanischer Garten Berlin runs from 18 November 2026 to 10 January 2027.
- Listed closed dates: 23, 24 and 30 November; 7, 24 and 31 December; 4 and 5 January.
- Normal listed last entry is 19:20 and final route start is 19:45.
- Friday and Saturday from 18 December 2026 to 2 January 2027 list last entry at 20:20 and final route start at 20:45.
  - Primary source: https://www.christmas-garden.de/berlin/besuch/
  - Event overview: https://www.christmas-garden.de/berlin/
- VBB is the live route-planning source.
  - Source: https://www.vbb.de/en/driving-information/apps/vbb-app-bus-bahn/

## Deliberately excluded

- Prices, ticket availability, sold-out dates, exchanges, route length, weather policy or accessibility claims.
- A generic availability result, calendar/month grid, fixed city-centre departure time or return promise.
- Any claim that a selected time is the visitor’s own booked entry window.
