Berlin Second Home Tax Ledger

Berlin taxes a flat or a WG room that is registered as your Nebenwohnung at 20 percent of its net cold rent, and since 2025 that includes subtenants paying for a share of someone else's flat. Put in your flat and the day it became your second address, and this adds up every month the Finanzamt will bill you for. Checked on 24 September 2026.

The flat, and how much of it is yours

If you rent the whole flat alone, put the full floor area as your own rooms and 0 shared. In a WG the law splits the kitchen, bathroom and hall equally between everyone who uses them and adds your own room on top.

Kaltmiete without heating and service charges.
Kitchen, bathroom, hall, shared living room.

When it became your second address

The tax follows your registration, not your lease. It starts on the first day of the month after the flat became your Nebenwohnung, or that same day if it was the first. It stops at the end of the month before the change if you re-register it as your main residence or move out.
