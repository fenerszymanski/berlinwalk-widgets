# Source notes — Tag der Clubkultur format filter

## Confirmed 2026 context

- Tag der Clubkultur’s official 2026 English page gives the festival-week dates as **3–11 October 2026** and the motto as **STAY CORE**.
- The same official material describes a citywide festival week with **100+ events** and the **32 recipients** of the 2026 cultural award.

## Deliberate boundaries

- This tool does not assert that a particular format is scheduled on a particular date. The cards are preference lanes to take to the current programme.
- It does not name, rank or recommend venues; predict queues; describe door rules; or imply admission.
- No individual event time, ticket condition, accessibility detail or award-recipient claim is used as a decision input.
