When are your years for German citizenship complete?

Enter the first day you lived in Germany lawfully and every stay abroad. The clock follows § 12b of the citizenship act: up to six months away is fine, longer can reset it.

Usually the date on your first Anmeldung or your first permit, whichever came later.
