BerlinWalk tool

Berlin Station Exit Map

Berlin exit signs are named after streets, not after the thing you came to see. Pick a station and your destination, and the map shows which Ausgang puts you closest, and what every other exit costs you in metres.

Where are you heading?

Exit positions and the street each one opens onto come from OpenStreetMap station entrance data, checked on 22 August 2026. Distances are straight-line metres from the exit group to the destination, so real walking is a little longer wherever a road or a rail viaduct is in the way.

Signs change during building work. Berlin has a lot of building work. If the letter you want is boarded up, follow the street name instead.
