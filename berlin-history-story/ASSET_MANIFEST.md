## Optimisation pass, 2026-09-01

Every existing delivery raster above was re-encoded for delivery: longest edge capped at 1600 px,
JPEG quality 86, progressive, `optimize=True`. Two files (`1961-wall-build.jpg`,
`1982-brandenburg-gate-wall.jpg`) were already smaller than their re-encode and
were deliberately left byte-identical rather than inflated. Photo payload fell
from **16,41 MB to 2,26 MB**. Every output was decode-checked for a non-zero
mean brightness, so none of them is a black frame. The hashes in the table above
are the post-optimisation values.

The cover/prologue archive derivative was added from the same licensed 1652-based map
on 2026-09-01. It is 720x463 px, 95,330 bytes, and is used as a low-opacity
context layer in the cover and first chapter. The original 1600px plan remains
the full evidence image for War, refuge, rebuilding; the derivative does not
relabel that plan as a view of an earlier period.

Delivery also changed: photos now carry `data-src` and are hydrated per chapter by
`_syncPhotos`, because the photo stack lives inside the sticky stage and is
hidden with `opacity`, not `display`. A browser therefore treats all ten as
in-viewport and `loading="lazy"` alone defers nothing. Browser DOM readback at
the opening cover/prologue shows the eager archive derivative plus only the excavation image and the
wordmark hydrated; every later photo still lacks `src`. A conservative static
sum of HTML, runtime, related data, map data, wordmark, all three runtime WOFF2
fonts and the two hydrated prologue images is 1,080,858 bytes (1.081 MB), below
the 1.2 MB handoff budget. Browser PerformanceResourceTiming is unavailable in
the approved in-app surface, so this bound intentionally counts every listed
font instead of inferring a smaller transfer.
