## Diagram-only scenes

Scenes 1, 2, 5 and 10 use component-rendered diagrams. Scene 3 also has a diagram underneath its historic map. No locally recorded Molkenmarkt photograph or Cölln/Berlin map is used because the local record did not include a canonical source URL required for this V1 manifest.

## Font files

`assets/fonts/` contains the approved local editorial font files with their upstream OFL notices: Fraunces, Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono. The font licence notices remain in that folder.
