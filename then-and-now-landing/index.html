Why this walk is different

The missing buildings
are part of the story.

Berlin's oldest streets disappeared through war, demolition and rebuilding. Looking at today's squares does not tell you what stood there. A photograph does.

01 / SEE

An archive photo
at every stop.

I hold up a photograph of the place where you are standing. Compare the old streets, the palace and the museum ruins with what is in front of you.

You can see the change, instead of having to imagine it.

02 / ASK

A group small enough
for your questions.

I keep each walk to a maximum of 10 guests. Ask about the building, the photograph or something you noticed on the way.

You walk with your guide and can join the conversation.

03 / CONNECT

One route through
Berlin's changing centre.

Alexanderplatz, the palace site and Museum Island belong to the same story. I connect the places with the choices that changed them.

You leave with a clearer idea of why Berlin looks this way.

A look at one stop

The church survived.
The neighbourhood did not.

Marienkirche once stood among narrow streets and houses. Today it stands beside the TV Tower in an open square. On the walk, I show you the photograph and explain how this ground changed.

to Hackescher Markt.

Follow the ground where the old city stood. The walk moves from the GDR's rebuilt centre through the palace site and Museum Island.

    Alexanderplatz & Marienkirche

    See the GDR skyline and the medieval church. The archive prints show the old streets cleared around them.

    Compare the royal palace, the Palast der Republik and the building standing here today.

  4. 04

    The Spree & Hackescher Markt

    Cross Friedrichsbrücke, look back at the island and finish at Hackescher Markt, with cafés and restaurants nearby.

Before you book

A few practical answers.

Where do I meet you?

At the World Clock (Weltzeituhr) on Alexanderplatz. Arrive five minutes before your booked start and look for my green umbrella. The walk ends at Hackescher Markt.

What is included in the €25?

The guided walking tour with Yusuf and archive photographs shown at every stop. The walk takes about 2.5 hours and is in English. Museum tickets, food and transport are not included.

How much walking is involved?

About 3 km on foot, with 11 stops covering 16 places. Wear comfortable shoes and dress for the weather. If you have a specific access requirement, contact me before booking so I can discuss the route with you.

How big is the group?

A maximum of 10 guests. A walk needs at least two guests to run. If I cancel your walk, you receive a full refund.

Can I cancel or change my date?

Cancel at least 24 hours before the start for a full refund, or change to another date if there is space. Later cancellations are not refundable. If I cancel the walk, you receive a full refund.

Does the walk go inside museums?

This is a walking tour of the historic centre. The route includes the palace site and Museum Island, but it is not a museum visit and admission tickets are not included.

What if it rains?

The walk runs in light rain, so bring a jacket. If conditions make the walk unsafe and I cancel, you receive a full refund.

Can I ask a question before booking?

Yes. Contact me if you want to check whether the walk suits your visit or ask about a private group.
